    Distribution waterfall

    Whole-of-fund, 8% hurdle, full GP catch-up, 80/20 split

    LPs get capital back plus the 8% preferred return first. The GP then catches up to 20% of profits, and everything above that is split 80/20. A clawback returns any excess carry at fund end.Red Book
