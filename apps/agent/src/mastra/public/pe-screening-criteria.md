# PE screening criteria

Harrowgate scores every target and segment against eight criteria. For each, give a verdict (meets, partial, fails, unknown), the evidence with source refs, and the key uncertainty. Unknown is acceptable; guessing is not.

1. Scale: EBITDA of £10m to £60m. Evidence: filed accounts, management data in internalData, or revenue times a sourced margin. Show the calculation.
2. Cash conversion: operating cash flow or (EBITDA less capex) over EBITDA, ideally above 80%. Evidence: cash flow statements. Flag heavy capitalised development or working-capital swings.
3. Recurring revenue: share of revenue that is contracted, subscription or repeat. Evidence: contract length, renewal and churn data, customer concentration. Project work does not count as recurring.
4. Margin: EBITDA margin against segment peers. Evidence: peer accounts and filings; note labour-heavy models cap margins.
5. Growth: organic revenue CAGR, separated from acquired growth. Evidence: multi-year accounts; compute with the calculator.
6. Moat and AI resilience: switching costs, regulatory mandate, proprietary data, workflow embedding. Assess how far generative AI or automation could replace the labour, the pricing model or the product. Cite named AI competitors or adoption evidence, not opinion.
7. Buy-and-build potential: number of fragmented targets, typical add-on multiples, and integration complexity. Evidence: sponsor activity and deal comps.
8. Entry price versus segment median: the likely EV/EBITDA against the median of recent comps in the segment. Only compare like with like (platform versus platform, LTM versus LTM).

Summarise with a count of criteria met, the one or two deal-breakers, and what diligence would resolve the unknowns.
